## body
How much is a square metre of flat in 2026? About €2,100 in Tirana, €1,500 in Durrës, €2,700 in Vlorë, and between €1,600 and €3,500 in Sarandë. These are averages and asking prices; inside one city the spread is wider than between cities. In Tirana a new flat in Blloku asks €3,000–5,500 per m², while Kombinat starts at €1,000. Below, each city district by district, with the source of every figure: Deloitte's averages, Keydata's recorded sales for Tirana, Domlivo's research base and the median of the listings on Domlivo.

## Durrës

Durrës has the most listings on the coast, so its medians are the firmest. On 18.10.2026 the median asking price was €1,500 per m² for the city, €1,611 in Plazh (148 flats), €1,376 in Golem (139), €1,404 in Shkëmbi i Kavajës (72) and €1,616 in the city centre (66). The seafront of the centre, Currila and Vollga, asks €1,900–2,800 per m²; Golem's first line €2,000–2,500. The month-by-month changes are in our [Durrës asking-price index](/blog/durres-asking-price-index-2026-10).

## Vlorë

Vlorë has two markets. The Lungomare, the seafront promenade, asks €2,500–3,500 per m²; the second line behind it €2,000–2,500. Jonufër and Radhimë, south of the city, ask €2,000–3,000 for newer complexes with a view, Orikum €1,300–1,500, and the city centre from €800 to €1,800. See [Vlorë by zone](/albania/vlore/info).

## Sarandë

Sarandë asks €1,600–1,800 per m² in the centre, €2,000–2,500 for new flats within a kilometre of the sea and €3,000–3,500 on the promenade, where resort apartments reach €4,000. Ksamil ranges from €1,800 to €3,000. The 11 flats listed on Domlivo on 18.10.2026 asked a median €2,041 per m². See [Sarandë by zone](/albania/sarande/info).

## Market price and reference price

The reference price the state uses for taxes is much lower than these figures: in Durrës 67,500 lekë per m², according to the press, about half of what flats there ask. What applies in each city in 2026 is in our article on [reference prices by city](/blog/albania-reference-prices-2026-by-city).

## faq

**How much is a square metre in Tirana in 2026?**
About €2,100 on Deloitte's 2025 average, with a wide spread: a new flat in Blloku asks €3,000–5,500 per m², while Kombinat starts at €1,000. Recorded sales in Q2 2025 averaged €2,054 per m² inside the Small Ring and €1,768 outside it.

**How much is a square metre in Durrës?**
The 511 flats and studios listed on Domlivo on 18.10.2026 asked a median €1,500 per m². By district: €1,611 in Plazh, €1,376 in Golem, €1,404 in Shkëmbi i Kavajës and €1,616 in the city centre. Deloitte's 2025 average for Durrës is about €1,650.

**How much is a square metre in Vlorë?**
On the Lungomare €2,500–3,500, on the second line €2,000–2,500, in Jonufër and Radhimë €2,000–3,000, and in the city centre from €800. Deloitte's 2025 average for Vlorë is about €2,700, the highest of the four cities.

**How much is a square metre in Sarandë?**
In the centre €1,600–1,800, for new flats near the sea €2,000–2,500, and on the promenade €3,000–3,500. Ksamil ranges from €1,800 to €3,000. The 11 flats listed on Domlivo on 18.10.2026 asked a median €2,041 per m².

**Are these sale prices or asking prices?**
Mostly asking prices: our research base and the Domlivo median measure what sellers ask. Deloitte's averages and Keydata's Tirana figures come from market data on sales. Albania publishes no official sale price per m² by city.
