## body
Can you buy a legalised building in Albania safely? Yes, if its property card is clean, and two entries decide that. A **statutory mortgage** (hipotekë ligjore) means the price of the plot under the building has not been paid yet. A registration **without the land** (pa marrëdhënie me truallin) means the plot belongs to someone else who has not yet been compensated by the state. Either one changes what you are buying. Below is how legalisation works under Law 20/2020, what to look for on the card, which documents to ask the seller for, and what the checks cost.

## How legalisation works

Law 20/2020 on completing transitional ownership processes replaced Law 9482/2006, under which the former agency ALUIZNI legalised informal buildings; the cadastre agency ASHK has done it since. The law covers buildings without a permit that appear on the national orthophoto taken before it entered into force. Buildings without an application are handled by ASHK on its own initiative.

Some buildings cannot be legalised at all: those that harm major public infrastructure or national roads, stand on cultural heritage, or lie in areas for strategic investment or on the coastline reserved for tourism. Applications need a site plan, floor plans and, for buildings above four floors, extensions or permit violations, a structural expertise.

ASHK decides within 60 days of completing the qualification, or within 30 days for a fee. The legalisation act also transfers the land when the owner of the building does not own the plot: for a home, up to three times the building's footprint and at most 500 m².

## The two entries that matter

**A statutory mortgage.** The plot under a legalised home is paid for at 50% of the land value for its cadastral zone, under Decision 446 of 26.07.2023. ASHK sends the payment notice through e-Albania or by post, and paying within 30 days earns a 20% discount. Until the plot is fully paid a statutory mortgage stays registered on the property. If you buy with it in place, the debt stays with the property; agree in the contract that the seller pays it before the deed, and see the mortgage lifted on the card.

**A registration without the land.** When the plot belonged to a private third party, ownership of the land passes only when the Council of Ministers decision compensating that owner takes effect. Until then the building is registered without its land. You can own and register such a building, but you do not own the ground under it, and the timing of compensation is not in your hands. In 2025 the government approved compensation for 952 properties, a total of 2,201,738,902.9 lekë.

## Step by step before you pay

1. **Get a fresh property card.** On e-Albania, a copy of the property card costs 1,500 lekë and comes with the cadastral map fragment. The card shows the owner, the property number, the zone and every encumbrance.
2. **Read the encumbrances.** Look for a statutory mortgage, a bank mortgage, a seizure or a court claim. Ask the seller to clear each one before the deed.
3. **Check whether the land is included.** Look for the note that the building is registered without the land.
4. **Ask for the legalisation act** and the receipt for the plot payment. The two should match the card.
5. **Compare the building with the documents.** Floors or extensions added after legalisation are not legal; the card and the act state what was legalised.
6. **Check the legal status online.** The online check of a property's legal status costs 1,000 lekë; an ownership certificate 3,000 lekë and an ownership confirmation 2,000.

At the notary, a contract transferring ownership of a property after legalisation costs 3,000 lekë. A lawyer who checks the title costs more but is the main protection: land and property cases make up a large share of civil cases in Albanian courts, and they take years.

## How far legalisation has gone

From 2006 to 2025, first ALUIZNI and then ASHK issued 291,114 final legalisation decisions, according to ASHK's 2025 report. The busiest year was 2016 with 48,351; in 2025 there were 26,101, more than in any year from 2019 to 2024. ASHK's 2025 annual report says initial registration, including legalisation, is to be finished in 2028. According to the press, about 130,000 self-declarations were still untreated in mid-2026, and a draft amendment published in September 2026 would, in zones planned for buildings above six floors, legalise only the dwelling and not pass the land to its owner. Neither figure comes from an official text we could open.

## faq

**Is it safe to buy a legalised apartment or house in Albania?**
Yes, if the property card is clean. Check that it shows no statutory mortgage, which means the plot has not been paid for, and no registration without the land, which means a third party still owns the plot. Ask for the legalisation act and the plot payment receipt.

**What is a statutory mortgage on a legalised property?**
A charge that ASHK registers until the owner pays for the plot under the building, at 50% of the land value for a home. It stays on the property when it is sold, so the contract should require the seller to pay it and have it lifted before the deed.

**What does "registered without the land" mean?**
The building stands on a plot that belonged to a private third party who has not yet been compensated. Ownership of the land passes only when the Council of Ministers' compensation decision takes effect. Until then you can buy and register the building, but not the land.

**How do I check a property's legal status online?**
On e-Albania: a copy of the property card with the map fragment costs 1,500 lekë, an online check of legal status 1,000 lekë, an ownership confirmation 2,000 and a certificate 3,000. The card lists the owner, the zone and every encumbrance.

**Can any unpermitted building still be legalised?**
Only buildings on the orthophoto taken before Law 20/2020 entered into force, and never those on the protected coastline, on cultural heritage, near major infrastructure or in strategic investment areas. ASHK plans to finish legalisation in 2028.
