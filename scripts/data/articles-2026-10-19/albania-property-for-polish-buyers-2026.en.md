## body
Can a Pole buy a flat in Albania? Yes, on the same terms as an Albanian citizen; only agricultural land is closed to foreigners, who may lease it for up to 99 years. Homes in Albania cost about half of what they cost in Poland: Deloitte's 2025 averages are €1,720 per m² for Albania and €3,008 for a new flat in Poland. Rent from an Albanian flat is taxed in Albania, and the Polish–Albanian tax treaty of 1993 exempts it in Poland. Below are the prices, the taxes on both sides and the steps of a purchase.

## Entry and stay

According to the Polish foreign ministry, a Pole enters Albania with an identity card or a passport valid for the whole stay and at least three months after leaving. No visa is needed for 90 days within any 180, and there is no duty to register. For a longer stay the ministry advises contacting the immigration office in advance; the application needs a passport, a criminal record certificate and proof of an address in Albania, for example a property deed or a lease. Owning property is also a ground for a one-year renewable residence permit under Article 84 of Albania's Law 79/2021.

## Buying, step by step

1. **Reservation.** A deposit (kaparë) of 5–10% of the price; developers ask up to 30% for off-plan flats.
2. **Checks.** Property card (kartela), encumbrances, cadastral map, building permit and occupancy permit. A lawyer is not compulsory but advisable; €500–2,000.
3. **Preliminary contract.** For an off-plan flat, only a notarised kontratë porosie registered with the cadastre (ASHK) protects you against a double sale.
4. **Notarial deed.** The only valid form of sale. The price goes by bank transfer; if you do not speak Albanian, a sworn translator co-signs.
5. **Registration.** The notary files with ASHK; in practice two to four weeks.

With clean documents the whole process takes four to eight weeks.

## What the purchase costs

On a €100,000 flat the buyer typically pays about €2,300: the notary (about 0.30% of the price), cadastre registration (5,000 lek) and the notary's filing request (4,000 lek), a lawyer, a translator and the agent's 1%. The 2% transfer tax is legally the seller's but is sometimes passed on in the contract; agree on it in writing. Apartment sales carry no VAT.

## Taxes in Albania

- **Rent.** 15%: on the gross rent for a long let; for a holiday let through Airbnb or Booking, on the income after the platform's commission, declared in the DIVA system by 31 March. Since 01.01.2026 a private owner needs no business registration for this.
- **Annual building tax.** 0.05% of the fiscal value (the zone's reference price times the area), usually a few thousand lek a year.
- **Sale.** 15% of the gain, measured against at least the zone's reference price. Until 31.12.2026 an owner can revalue the property and pay 5% on the increase instead.

## Taxes in Poland

The Polish–Albanian tax treaty was signed in Warsaw on 5 March 1993 (Journal of Laws 1994 no. 101 item 492) and has applied since 1995. Albania may tax rent from Albanian property (Article 6) and the gain when you sell it (Article 13). Poland exempts that income and may take it into account when it sets the rate on your other income (Article 24(1)(a) and (c)). The credit method applies only to dividends, interest and royalties. Albania opted out of the multilateral convention's article on methods, so, on our reading, the treaty's exemption still applies in 2026.

In Poland, private rental income has been taxed since 2023 only as a lump sum and declared on the PIT-28 return by 30 April. How exempt Albanian rent is shown on that return is a question we could not confirm from the tax authority's pages; ask a tax adviser before your first return.

## faq

**Can a Pole buy a flat in Albania?**
Yes. A Polish citizen buys an apartment, a house or commercial property on the same terms as an Albanian, with the right to sell it and leave it to heirs. Agricultural land is the exception: foreigners may lease it for up to 99 years but not own it.

**How much is a flat in Albania compared with Poland?**
About half. Deloitte's 2025 averages are €1,720 per m² for a dwelling in Albania and €3,008 for a new flat in Poland. On the Albanian coast the flats listed on Domlivo asked a median €1,500 per m² in Durrës and €2,041 in Sarandë on 18.10.2026.

**Do I pay tax in Poland on rent from an Albanian flat?**
Under the 1993 treaty Albania taxes the rent at 15% and Poland exempts it, though it may count it when setting the rate. Private rent in Poland is declared on PIT-28; how the exempt Albanian rent appears there is worth confirming with a tax adviser.

**How long can a Pole stay in Albania without a visa?**
90 days within any 180, with an identity card or a passport valid at least three months after departure, according to the Polish foreign ministry. A longer stay needs a residence permit; owning property is one of the legal grounds for it.

**How long does buying a flat in Albania take?**
Four to eight weeks with clean documents: one to two weeks of checks, one day at the notary and two to four weeks for registration with the cadastre. An off-plan flat must be reserved through a notarised contract registered with the cadastre.
