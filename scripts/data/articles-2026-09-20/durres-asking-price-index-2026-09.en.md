## body
On 20 September 2026 Domlivo carried 358 active sale listings in Durrës, 268 of them flats: apartments, studios and penthouses. The median asking price of a flat was €100,000, and the median asking price per square metre was €1,413. The cheapest flat was listed at €40,000. These are asking prices taken from live listings, not recorded sale prices.

The median total price is taken over the 210 flats priced as a total. The median per square metre is taken over 263 flats, because it also counts flats that are priced per m².

The one-bedroom flat is the market here: 151 of the 268 flats are 1+1. The 3+1 row rests on 11 flats, so its low figure per m² is a small-sample reading. The cheapest flat in the city, at €40,000, is a 2+1.

## Near the sea

The 89 flats that are on the first line or within 300 m of the sea have a median asking price of €105,000 and a median of €1,400 per m², with the cheapest at €50,000. The city medians are €100,000 and €1,413 per m².

The limitation matters more than the gap. Sea distance is recorded for only 100 of the 268 flats, and 89 of those 100 are near the sea. The comparison therefore says almost nothing about flats far from the water, and it should not be read as a measured sea premium. The listings are on the page for [flats near the sea in Durrës](/albania/durres/near-the-sea).

## New builds

The sample has 45 flats sold off-plan or under construction. Their median asking price is €83,300, their median per square metre €1,250, and the cheapest is listed at €59,360. Against the city median of €1,413 per m², unfinished flats in this sample are listed lower. See the current [new builds in Durrës](/albania/durres/new-builds).

## Method and limits

- These are asking prices from the listings of one marketplace, Domlivo. They are not transaction prices, and a final price agreed between buyer and seller can differ.
- The sample is every published, active sale listing in Durrës on 20 September 2026. Flats are apartments, studios and penthouses.
- The inventory comes largely from partner agencies and is concentrated in certain districts: Golem, Plazh and the City Center hold most of the listings. A district with few listings can move a lot from one month to the next without any change in the market.
- All figures are medians, not means, so a few very expensive flats do not pull them up.
- The price per m² is the total price divided by the area, or the stated rate where a flat is priced per m². A district is reported from 3 flats.
- This is the first edition, so there is no earlier index to compare with and nothing here says whether prices are rising or falling.

The same arithmetic runs on the site every hour: the live table is on the [Durrës prices page](/albania/durres/info), and all current listings are under [property for sale in Durrës](/albania/durres). The index will be republished every month.

## faq
**How much does a one-bedroom flat in Durrës cost in 2026?**
€90,000 is the median asking price of a one-bedroom flat (1+1) in Durrës on 20 September 2026, across 151 listings on Domlivo. The cheapest 1+1 was listed at €55,000, and the median asking price per square metre was €1,416. These are asking prices from live listings, not recorded sale prices.

**Which part of Durrës is cheapest?**
Shkëmbi has the lowest median asking price for a flat, €82,000, followed by Qerret at €85,000, Mali i Robit at €87,000 and Golem at €88,000. Per square metre, Qerret (€1,300) and Golem (€1,311) are the lowest. The City Center is the most expensive by total price, at €140,000.

**Are these sale prices?**
No. They are asking prices: what sellers and agencies wrote on 358 active listings on Domlivo on 20 September 2026. The price finally agreed between buyer and seller can differ from the listing, and this index does not see it. Use the figures to compare districts and sizes, not as a valuation of a specific flat.

**What is the asking price per square metre in Durrës?**
€1,413 per m² is the median asking price for a flat in Durrës on 20 September 2026, measured over 263 flats. By district it runs from €1,300 in Qerret to €1,625 in Plepa, where the sample is only 7 flats. Flats sold off-plan or under construction have a median of €1,250 per m².

**How often is the index updated?**
Monthly. This September 2026 edition is the first, so it has no earlier edition to compare with and makes no claim about prices rising or falling. Between editions, the live table on the Durrës prices page recalculates the same medians every hour from the listings that are active at that moment.
