## Title
Off-Plan, Completed, Old Stock or Land? Rating Albanian Property Types by Risk

## keyFacts
1. Albania has no escrow protection for off-plan purchases, so a buyer's deposit sits with the developer rather than with a neutral third party.
2. The typical off-plan discount of 20-30% against a completed unit is best read as the price of carrying that risk, not as free money.
3. A completed new build holding its leje përdorimi - the certificate of use - is on our assessment the best risk-adjusted purchase available in the market.
4. Penthouses carry a 20-40% price premium that rental income does not recover, so the case for one is lifestyle rather than yield.
5. Pre-2000 stock in seismic zones carries a permanent discount after the 2019 earthquake, and that discount is rational rather than an opportunity.

## excerpt
Where you buy in Albania gets discussed constantly. What you buy gets discussed far less, and it changes the risk profile more. Here is how we rate each property type in the Albanian market as of 2026 - off-plan against completed, new against old stock, penthouses, villas, townhouses and land - and what has to be true for each one to make sense.

## Body
Two buyers in the same Durrës building can end up with completely different outcomes because one bought a finished apartment with its paperwork in order and the other bought a floor plan. The zone was identical. The risk was not.

This is our type-by-type assessment for 2026. Confidence is moderate: these are judgements about structure and incentives, informed by market data, not guarantees about individual buildings.

#### Off-plan, bought at foundation stage

Approach with caution, and only with developers whose completion record you have personally verified.

The structural issue is that Albania has no escrow requirement. In markets with escrow, your money sits with a neutral party and is released against construction milestones. In Albania it goes to the developer, who is also the party whose solvency you are betting on. If the project stalls, you are an unsecured creditor of a construction company.

The 20-30% discount to a completed unit is real, and it is not a gift. It is the market pricing that risk. The question to ask is not whether the discount is attractive but whether this specific developer has delivered comparable projects on time, and what happens to your deposit if they do not.

Buying off-plan in 2026 has one additional consideration: transaction volumes are down about 35% year on year, which is exactly the environment in which pre-sale-funded developers run short of cash.

#### Completed new build with leje përdorimi

This is the best risk-adjusted purchase in the market, and it is where we would put a first-time buyer without hesitation.

The leje përdorimi — the certificate of use — is the document that confirms the building was completed lawfully and may be occupied. With it, the construction risk is gone, the unit can be rented immediately, and the title path is clean. The premium over off-plan is genuinely earned.

If you take one thing from this article: ask for the leje përdorimi by name, and do not accept "it is coming" as an answer.

#### Secondary stock built before 2000

Avoid in seismic zones. Treat with care in Tirana.

The 2019 earthquake changed how Albanians price older construction, and it did so permanently. Pre-2000 buildings were largely built under standards and supervision that no longer inspire confidence, and the discount they carry reflects a real difference in structural risk rather than a market inefficiency waiting to be arbitraged.

In central Tirana some older stock is worth owning for location and plot value. On the coast, in the zones that shook, we would not.

#### Penthouses

Neutral, with a clear warning about the arithmetic.

Penthouses command a 20-40% premium. Rental income does not rise proportionally — tenants pay for bedrooms and location far more than for a top floor and a terrace. That makes the penthouse premium a lifestyle purchase that happens to be in an investment article.

Buy one because you want to live in it. Do not buy one expecting the premium back on exit unless the building is genuinely prime.

#### Villas

Positive, for capital rather than income.

Villas in Lalzi Bay, Farkë and along the Riviera sit in a genuinely thin segment. Supply is limited, and demand comes from the diaspora and from European buyers with specific requirements, which is a more durable buyer pool than speculative apartment demand.

The trade-off is liquidity: fewer buyers means a longer sale. This is a hold, not a flip.

#### Townhouses

Neutral, mostly because the market is thin.

There is nothing structurally wrong with the product. There are simply not enough transactions to establish a reliable price or a dependable exit, which makes it hard to know what you are buying at and harder to know what you will sell at.

#### Land

The highest upside on this list and the only one we would not touch without a lawyer.

The issues are title history, agricultural classification and restitution claims — the last being the legacy of ownership transfers going back decades that remain unresolved in parts of the country. Any of the three can make a plot unbuildable or unsellable, and none of them are visible from a photograph.

Handled properly, with the legal work done before money moves, land offers the largest gains available in this market. Handled casually, it is the fastest way to lose the entire amount.

#### Commercial and street retail

An open question rather than a recommendation.

We do not have enough reliable data on Albanian street retail and offices to give a view worth acting on. Published office vacancy in Tirana has been reported at around 12.55%, which is a number, but a single vacancy figure is not an investment case. We would rather say we do not know than fill the gap with confident prose.

#### The short version

If you want one rule: pay the premium for a completed building with its certificate of use, and spend what you save on a lawyer rather than on a bigger terrace.

## faq
**Is buying off-plan in Albania safe?**
It carries a specific, structural risk: Albania has no escrow requirement, so your deposit goes to the developer rather than to a neutral third party, and if the project stalls you rank as an unsecured creditor. The 20-30% discount to a finished unit is the market pricing that risk rather than a bargain. It can work with a developer whose completion record you have verified yourself, and it is a poor choice for a first purchase.

**What is leje përdorimi and why does it matter?**
It is the certificate of use — the document confirming a building was lawfully completed and may be occupied. A unit that holds one carries no construction risk, can be rented immediately and has a clean path to title. It is the single most useful thing to ask for by name, and "it is coming" is not an acceptable answer.

**Are old apartments in Albania a bargain?**
Usually not in seismic zones. The discount on pre-2000 stock reflects genuine differences in construction standards, and the 2019 earthquake made that permanent rather than temporary. In central Tirana some older buildings are worth owning for the location and the plot; on the coast, in the areas that shook, we would not treat the discount as an opportunity.

**Is land a good investment in Albania?**
It offers the largest upside available in this market and the largest chance of total loss. Title history, agricultural classification and unresolved restitution claims can each make a plot unbuildable or unsellable, and none are visible without legal research. It is viable only with a lawyer engaged before any money moves.
