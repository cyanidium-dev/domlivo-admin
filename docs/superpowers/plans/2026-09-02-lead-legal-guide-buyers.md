## lead
Three taxes and one deadline. Housing is taxed at 0.05% of fiscal value a year, roughly 3,600 to 10,200 lek for a 90 square metre Tirana flat. Selling costs 15% capital gains, and short-term rental income has been taxed at 15% since January 2026. Until 31 December 2026 you can revalue at 5% instead.
