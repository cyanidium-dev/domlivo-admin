# Blog Lead Patch — `tirana-apartment-prices-2026`

## lead
Tirana apartments roughly doubled in six years, from about 1,000 euros per square metre in 2020 to 1,863 euros by the second quarter of 2025. Almost the entire move landed in two of those years, 2023 through the second half of 2024, which alone ran 56% year on year. Then the market went flat.
