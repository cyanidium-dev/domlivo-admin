# Blog Lead Patch — `durres-documents-checklist`

## lead
Ask for the kartela e pasurisë first and pull it from ASHK yourself: it is the one document that proves who owns the property and which encumbrances sit on it. Then the leje përdorimi for any finished building, a notarised and registered kontrata e porosisë for off-plan, and the encumbrance extract on the parent plot, not just the unit.
