# Blog Lead Patch — `best-districts-tirana`

## lead
Komuna e Parisit and Myslym Shyri for families, both rated 8/10 on parks, schools and walkability. Blloku and Pazari i Ri for nightlife and the strongest Airbnb demand, at the cost of noise and price. Farkë-Lundër and the Rruga e Elbasanit corridor for gated villas, and Don Bosko and Fresku for gentrifying value.
